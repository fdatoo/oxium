---

Every domain term used in Phase 1 of *How Oxium Builds a World*, alphabetical. Links point to the chapter that introduces or canonically defines the term.

---

### A

**Aquifer.** A region of subterranean fluid (Water or Lava) defined by a jittered 3D Voronoi cell grid, each cell 16 × 12 × 16 blocks. Each active cell holds a `y_top` (water-table height) and a fluid kind; at chunk fill time any carved voxel at or below `y_top` is flooded by the pressure model. Only 3 % of cells are active (`keep_chance = 0.03`); the rest are permanently dry. See [4.7 Aquifers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.7-aquifers.mdx).

**ArcSwap.** The atomic-swap pointer type from the `arc_swap` crate. The engine wraps `WorldgenConfig` in `Arc<ArcSwap<WorldgenConfig>>` inside `ConfigHolder`, letting the file watcher atomically replace the config while chunk-fill threads read it lock-free via `load_full()`. See [5.3 Config & Hot-Reload](../part-5-engineering/5.3-config-hot-reload.mdx).

**Asymmetric pressure.** The aquifer model's function for deciding whether dense rock or fluid wins at a voxel that the density field marks solid but the aquifer wants to flood. Pressure drops quadratically above `y_top` (`−top_falloff × gap²`) and rises linearly below it (capped at +1). Rock holds when `density > pressure + barrier_noise`. See [4.7 Aquifers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.7-aquifers.mdx).

**Avalanche.** The property of a hash function where flipping one input bit flips roughly half the output bits. Achieved in the engine by the three-step xor-shift + multiply finaliser in `hash::mix`. Essential for preventing visible terrain patterns from correlated integer coordinates. See [1.2 Deterministic Randomness](../part-1-foundations/1.2-determinism.mdx).

---

### B

**Base 3D noise.** The `DensityFn::BaseNoise3D` leaf in the density graph — a 3-octave Simplex FBM seeded at `seed + 701`, with a Y-axis scale of 0.5 that stretches vertical features. Appears twice in the default tree: once multiplied by the jaggedness spline to add roughness, and once added directly after `composition_scale` to contribute fine surface texture. See [4.1 The Density Tree](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.1-density-graph.mdx).

**Bilinear interpolation (bilerp).** Smooth blending of four corner values across a 2D rectangular patch: lerp along X at both Y values, then lerp along Y. Used in the 2D projection of trilinear interpolation. Three lerp operations total. See [1.9 Trilinear Interpolation](../part-1-foundations/1.9-trilerp.mdx).

**Biome.** A named region type (`Tundra`, `SnowyForest`, `Plains`, `Forest`, `Desert`, `Tropical`) selected by nearest-neighbour lookup in a six-dimensional climate space. The lookup structure is a `Vec<ParameterPoint>` with a hand-built R-tree for pruning. Biome governs surface block choice, tree rate, and tree kind, but is fully decoupled from the noise fields that generate climate. See [3.2 Climate](../part-3-region-build/3.2-climate.mdx).

**Block.** A `#[repr(u16)]` enum representing a single voxel's content: `Air`, `Stone`, `Dirt`, `Grass`, `Sand`, `Water`, `Wood`, `Leaves`, `Torch`, `Snow`, `Lava`. The discriminant is the on-disk wire format; its ordering is fixed. See [1.1 The Voxel World](../part-1-foundations/1.1-voxel-world.mdx).

---

### C

**Carver tunnel.** A procedural cave structure generated by `carver::build_tunnels_for_chunk`: a chain of ellipsoidal spheres traced by a random walk of up to 112 steps, with horizontal and vertical radii that follow a sine-profile over the walk's lifetime (widest at midpoint) and a flat-floor cutoff. Tunnels from a chunk's own seed and from all chunks within a ±5/±2 XZ/Y neighbourhood are rasterised into a per-chunk boolean mask before the voxel loop runs. See [4.5 Procedural Carvers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.5-procedural-carvers.mdx).

**Catmull-Rom spline.** A spline variant where each knot's derivative is derived automatically from its neighbours (unlike cubic Hermite, where derivatives are authored explicitly). The graph cave system comments describe stored tunnel control points as "a Catmull-Rom spline through 2–4 control points," approximated at carve time as a sequence of capsule segments. See [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx).

**Cave system (graph form).** A per-region set of chambers (Poisson-disk placed ellipsoids), connecting tunnels (MST + extra loops), and surface entrances (sinkhole, cliff mouth, or skylight), stored in `FineRegion::cave_systems`. Currently disabled (`CAVE_SYSTEMS_PER_REGION = (0, 0)`) in favour of the procedural carver. See [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx).

**Cell evaluator.** The `CellEvaluator` struct in `density_graph.rs`. It builds a 9×9×9 corner lattice (729 samples) at the start of each chunk by evaluating the full `DensityFn` tree once per corner, then answers per-voxel density queries via trilinear interpolation (7 lerps). Yields a ~134× reduction in expensive density evaluations per chunk. See [4.2 The Cell Evaluator](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.2-cell-evaluator.mdx).

**Chamber.** A single air-filled ellipsoid in a graph cave system, stored as `Chamber { center: Vec3, radii: Vec3 }` with independently drawn semi-axes in `[3.5, 5.5]` blocks. Chambers are placed by Poisson-disk sampling within a system bounding box, connected by a minimum spanning tree of tunnels. See [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx).

**ChunkCoord.** A newtype over `glam::IVec3` of integer chunk indices. Each chunk spans `CHUNK_DIM = 32` blocks per axis; `ChunkCoord(n, m, p)` covers world coordinates `[32n..32(n+1)] × [32m..32(m+1)] × [32p..32(p+1)]`. See [1.1 The Voxel World](../part-1-foundations/1.1-voxel-world.mdx).

**Coherent noise.** A continuous scalar function over real-valued coordinates that varies smoothly, looks random at scale, and has no discontinuities. The engine uses Simplex noise (via the `noise` crate) as its coherent noise primitive. Contrast with `hash::mix`, which produces uncorrelated randomness. See [1.3 Coherent Noise](../part-1-foundations/1.3-coherent-noise.mdx).

**Composition scale.** The `composition_scale` constant (default 4.0) in `DensityConfig`. Applied as a multiplier to the `shaped` term before the final addition of `base_3d`. Amplifying the shaped signal before the post-composition noise addition makes the surface transition sharper everywhere. See [4.1 The Density Tree](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.1-density-graph.mdx).

**ConfigHolder.** The hot-reload handle `ConfigHolder(Arc<ArcSwap<WorldgenConfig>>)`. Exposes `load()` (atomic lock-free snapshot, returns `Arc<WorldgenConfig>`) and `swap()` (atomically replaces the inner pointer). The generator and the file watcher share a cloned `ConfigHolder`. See [5.3 Config & Hot-Reload](../part-5-engineering/5.3-config-hot-reload.mdx).

**Continentalness.** A signed scalar field in `[-1.1, 1.1]` derived from the plate Voronoi decomposition: strongly positive over continental cores, strongly negative under open ocean, near zero at coastlines. The outermost axis of the three nested splines that drive the heightmap. Computed by `signed_continentalness(&PlateLookup)` in `heightmap.rs`. See [3.1 Plates](../part-3-region-build/3.1-plates.mdx) and [3.3 Heightmap](../part-3-region-build/3.3-heightmap.mdx).

**Cubic Hermite spline.** The spline variant used throughout the engine. Each knot has an explicit `loc` (input position), `val` (output value), and `slope` (derivative). Between two knots the interpolation follows a cubic polynomial that matches both values and both slopes exactly, with linear extrapolation outside the knot range. See [1.7 Splines](../part-1-foundations/1.7-splines.mdx).

### D

**D8 flow direction.** A per-cell scalar (0–7, or 8 = no outlet) recording which of the eight compass neighbours (cardinal and diagonal) is the steepest downhill path on the PD-filled heightmap. Diagonal steps are penalised by distance √2 so slope comparisons are geometrically fair. Used in the hydrology pass to build the river drainage tree. See [3.4 Hydrology](../part-3-region-build/3.4-hydrology.mdx).

**DenseChunk.** The working-memory chunk representation: a flat `Box<[Block; 32768]>` (32³) of `Block` values, indexed by `LocalPos::to_index()`. Random access is O(1) and fast; the mesher and lighting pass use this form. Long-term storage uses `PalettedChunk`. See [1.1 The Voxel World](../part-1-foundations/1.1-voxel-world.mdx).

**Density field.** The signed scalar value computed at each voxel by the `DensityFn` tree and the `CellEvaluator`. Positive means solid; negative (or zero) means air. All cave carving, aquifer flooding, and surface assignment downstream pivot on the sign of this number. See [4.1 The Density Tree](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.1-density-graph.mdx).

**DensityFn.** A recursive enum of nodes (`YGradient`, `BaseNoise3D`, `Spline`, `Add`, `Mul`, `QuarterNegative`, `Constant`, `Marker`, `Min`, `Max`) that represents the density computation as an expression tree. Built fresh each chunk from the live config snapshot by `build_default_tree`. See [4.1 The Density Tree](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.1-density-graph.mdx).

**Domain warping.** The technique of perturbing a function's *input coordinates* with a smooth offset field before sampling it, producing bent or curved output features. In the engine: river meanders (`perpendicular_distance` offsets the projected centreline point perpendicularly) and cave tunnel shaping (control points are displaced perpendicular to the tunnel axis). Classical 2D domain warping was used in an earlier heightmap version but replaced by splines. See [1.6 Domain Warping](../part-1-foundations/1.6-domain-warping.mdx).

**Drainage.** The per-cell accumulated count of upstream fine cells that flow through a given cell, computed by topological sort after D8 flow directions are assigned. Cells with `acc ≥ RIVER_THRESH (50)` are rivers; their width scales as `sqrt(acc) × RIVER_WIDTH_SCALE`. See [3.4 Hydrology](../part-3-region-build/3.4-hydrology.mdx).

### E

**Entrance (cave).** A deliberate opening connecting a graph cave system chamber to the surface. Three kinds: **Sinkhole** (vertical shaft, chamber top within 8 blocks of `h_pre`), **CliffMouth** (horizontal exit through a cliff face within 30 blocks), **Skylight** (narrow 1–2 block shaft, gap of 30–60 blocks between chamber top and surface). Entrances bypass the `CAVE_SURFACE_BUFFER` gate that protects the topmost solid layer. See [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx).

---

### F

**FBM (Fractional Brownian Motion).** A weighted sum of noise octaves at geometrically increasing frequencies and decreasing amplitudes. The engine always uses `Fbm::<Simplex>` from the `noise` crate. Three parameters: **octaves** (how many copies), **persistence** (amplitude decay per octave, default 0.5), **lacunarity** (frequency multiplier per octave, default 2.0). Output is normalised by dividing by the sum of amplitudes. See [1.4 Fractional Brownian Motion](../part-1-foundations/1.4-fbm.mdx).

**FineRegion.** A 512 × 512 block region of per-column cached worldgen data: `h_pre`, `flow_dir`, `flow_acc`, `is_river`, `is_lake`, `width`, `lake_rim` (all 64×64 cell arrays), plus `segments: Vec<RiverSegment>` and `cave_systems: Vec<CaveSystem>`. Built once per region coord and memoised in `FineCache` (capacity 256). `fill_chunk` pre-fetches a 3×3 grid of fine regions before starting per-column work. See [5.2 The Region Cache](../part-5-engineering/5.2-region-cache.mdx).

**`fluid::settle_fluid`.** A two-pass O(chunk_volume) filter that removes physically unsupported aquifer-placed fluid voxels. Phase 1 propagates support upward (solid below, supported masked-fluid below, or non-masked fluid below all count as support). Phase 2 converts every unsupported masked fluid voxel to Air. Does not simulate fluid spreading — only removes floating phantom blocks. See [4.8 Fluid Settle](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.8-fluid-settle.mdx).

---

### H

**`hash::mix`.** The engine's single-source pure hash function: `mix(seed: u64, words: &[i32]) -> u64`. Each word gets a distinct multiplier from a five-element table and a distinct rotation, making the function order-sensitive. A three-step xor-shift + multiply finaliser provides full avalanche. All discrete randomness in the worldgen descends from this function. See [1.2 Deterministic Randomness](../part-1-foundations/1.2-determinism.mdx).

**`h_pre`.** The pre-carve surface height of a column in world-Y blocks, produced by the heightmap spline pipeline. Computed per-column by `HeightmapNoise::h_pre(seed, wx, wz, cfg)`. Used as the baseline for D8 flow routing and subtracted from valley carve to produce `h_target`. See [3.3 Heightmap](../part-3-region-build/3.3-heightmap.mdx).

**`h_target`.** The final column surface height after valley carving: `h_target = (h_pre − valley_carve).clamp(CAVE_FLOOR_Y + 8, MAX_TERRAIN_Y)`. Used by surface rules, tree placement, biome depth axis, and aquifer lookups as the canonical column top. See [3.5 Rivers, Valleys, Lakes](../part-3-region-build/3.5-rivers-lakes.mdx).

---

### J

**Jaggedness spline.** One of the three nested splines in the heightmap pipeline. Keys on `(continentalness, terrain_shape, ridges_pv)` and returns a sharpness multiplier that scales the `jagged_rider = jaggedness × base_3d` term. Near zero on beaches; high on mountain ridges. Controls whether `ridges_pv` injects sharp ridgelines or the terrain stays smooth. See [3.3 Heightmap](../part-3-region-build/3.3-heightmap.mdx).

**Jittered grid.** A regular spatial grid where each cell contains exactly one Voronoi seed placed at a hash-derived position within the cell's interior (never forced to the center). Bounds the nearest-seed search to a 3×3 (or 3×3×3) window of cells regardless of world size. Used for plates (1024-block cells), aquifer cells (16×12×16 blocks), and tree cells (8×8 blocks). See [1.5 Voronoi Diagrams](../part-1-foundations/1.5-voronoi.mdx).

---

### K

**Kruskal's algorithm.** The minimum-spanning-tree algorithm used to wire cave system chambers into a connected tunnel graph. Edges (pairwise chamber distances) are sorted and added greedily, using union-find to skip edges that would form cycles. A small number of extra non-tree edges (`MST_EXTRA_LOOPS`) are added afterwards to create navigable loops. See [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx).

---

### L

**Lacunarity.** The frequency multiplier between successive FBM octaves. Default 2.0 (each octave has twice the frequency of the previous). Rarely tuned away from 2.0. See [1.4 Fractional Brownian Motion](../part-1-foundations/1.4-fbm.mdx).

**Lake rim.** The filled elevation of a lake basin, stored per fine cell as `lake_rim: Box<[i16]>`. Set to `h_fill` (the Planchon–Darboux-raised height) for any cell whose filled height exceeds its natural height. At chunk fill, any non-solid voxel at or below the rim becomes Water. The maximum rim in the 3×3 cell neighbourhood is used to avoid terracing at cell boundaries. See [3.5 Rivers, Valleys, Lakes](../part-3-region-build/3.5-rivers-lakes.mdx).

**LRU cache.** The eviction policy used by both the fine region cache (`FineCache`, capacity 256) and the macro region cache (`MacroCache`, capacity 32), and the carver tunnel cache (capacity 2048). Implemented via the `lru` crate. Entries are built outside the mutex on a miss and re-insertable without loss (builds are pure functions). See [5.2 The Region Cache](../part-5-engineering/5.2-region-cache.mdx).

---

### M

**MacroRegion.** A coarser-grid region (128 × 128 macro cells, one macro cell = 64 blocks, region = 8192 blocks) used for continental-scale trunk-river routing. Stores `flow_dir`, `flow_acc`, `is_trunk`, `is_lake`, `lake_rim` per macro cell. Macro trunk accumulation values are injected into the fine flow pass to give large-scale rivers their correct drainage area. See [3.4 Hydrology](../part-3-region-build/3.4-hydrology.mdx).

**Meander warp.** A domain-warp technique applied to river segments: a 1D hash-noise field along the segment's arc-length drives a perpendicular offset that bends the effective centreline before distance is measured. Amplitude scales with river width (`MEANDER_AMP_PER_WIDTH = 0.4`), capped at `MAX_MEANDER_AMP = 16.0` blocks, so trunk rivers curve significantly while streams stay nearly straight. See [3.5 Rivers, Valleys, Lakes](../part-3-region-build/3.5-rivers-lakes.mdx) and [1.6 Domain Warping](../part-1-foundations/1.6-domain-warping.mdx).

**MST (minimum spanning tree).** A connected acyclic subgraph spanning all chamber nodes in a cave system, built by Kruskal's algorithm on pairwise Euclidean distances. The MST guarantees every chamber is reachable with the minimum total tunnel length. Extra loop edges are added afterward to create cycles. See [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx).

---

### N

**NestedSpline.** A recursive enum (`Constant(f32)` or `Multipoint(Vec<NestedKnot>)`) used for the heightmap's three spline pipelines. The outermost level keys on continentalness; each knot's `val` is itself a `NestedSpline` keyed on terrain_shape; the innermost keys on ridges_pv. Evaluated by `evaluate_inner(&[c, s, r], depth)`. Lets a 2D function (height as a function of two climate axes) be expressed as nested 1D cubic Hermite splines without a 2D lookup table. See [3.3 Heightmap](../part-3-region-build/3.3-heightmap.mdx).

**NoiseCarvers.** The struct in `caves.rs` holding all FBM fields used by the four noise-cave channels: `cheese`, `cave_layer`, `spaghetti_2d`, `spaghetti_2d_modulator`, `spaghetti_2d_elevation`, `spaghetti_2d_thickness`, `spaghetti_roughness`, `pillar`, `pillar_rareness`, `pillar_thickness`, `surface_entrance`. Each field uses a unique seed salt (1001–1011). See [4.4 Noise Carvers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.4-noise-carvers.mdx).

---

### O

**Octave.** A single layer of coherent noise in an FBM stack, sampled at a specific frequency and amplitude. The `i`-th octave (0-indexed) has frequency `f₀ × lacunarity^i` and amplitude `persistence^i`. The engine uses 2–8 octaves depending on the field (climate: 2, terrain shape: 3, density relief: 3). See [1.4 Fractional Brownian Motion](../part-1-foundations/1.4-fbm.mdx).

---

### P

**PalettedChunk.** The compressed on-disk / idle-memory form of a chunk. Stores a `palette: Vec<Block>` (typically 1–6 entries) and `indices: Packed4Bit` (4 bits per voxel indexing into the palette). Before meshing or lighting, the engine decompresses to `DenseChunk`; after the worldgen write, it compresses back. See [1.1 The Voxel World](../part-1-foundations/1.1-voxel-world.mdx).

**ParameterPoint.** A single biome entry in the climate lookup table. Specifies a closed interval `[min, max]` on each of the six climate axes (temperature, humidity, continentalness, terrain_shape, depth, weirdness) plus a `biome` variant and an `offset` tie-breaker. The nearest `ParameterPoint` by squared-L2 distance-to-interval wins. Stored in a `Vec<ParameterPoint>` with a hand-built R-tree; biome lookup is a **linear scan within the R-tree's pruned subtrees**, not a raw linear scan of the whole list. See [3.2 Climate](../part-3-region-build/3.2-climate.mdx).

**Persistence.** The amplitude decay factor between successive FBM octaves. Default 0.5 (each octave has half the amplitude of the previous), ensuring high-frequency detail is visually subordinate to the base shape. See [1.4 Fractional Brownian Motion](../part-1-foundations/1.4-fbm.mdx).

**Planchon–Darboux (PD) sink fill.** An algorithm that raises local minima in a heightmap just enough for water to drain outward. Uses a min-priority queue seeded at boundary cells; interior cells are raised to at least the lowest path elevation to any boundary. Cells whose filled height exceeds their natural height are lake interiors. Run per fine region over a 5×5 region window. See [3.4 Hydrology](../part-3-region-build/3.4-hydrology.mdx).

**Plate / PlateKind / PlateLookup.** A **Plate** is a tectonic cell of 1024×1024 blocks, carrying `id: PlateId`, `kind: PlateKind` (Continental or Oceanic), `seed_xz: Vec2`, and `roughness: f32`. **PlateLookup** is the output of `plate_at(seed, wx, wz)`: nearest plate `a`, second-nearest `b`, distances `d_a` and `d_b`, and boundary intensity `t = (d_b - d_a) / (d_b + d_a)`. All plate properties are recomputed on demand; no plate registry exists. See [3.1 Plates](../part-3-region-build/3.1-plates.mdx).

**Poisson-disk sampling.** A point placement algorithm ensuring no two points are closer than a minimum separation. Used in cave system generation to place chambers: candidate centers are generated randomly inside the system bounding box and rejected if too close to any already-accepted chamber. Minimum spacing is `mean(rx, ry, rz) × POISSON_MIN_SPACING_MULT (3.0)` blocks. See [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx).

**Pressure model (aquifer).** The rule in `AquiferSystem::substance` that governs whether rock or fluid wins at a voxel where the density field wants solid but the aquifer water table is above. Combines `asymmetric_pressure(wy, y_top, top_falloff, bottom_falloff)` with a 3D FBM barrier noise sample; rock holds when `density > pressure + barrier`. At cell boundaries between differing fluid kinds a **unanimity check** on the three nearest cells prevents chaotic fluid mixing — only if all three agree does fluid win. See [4.7 Aquifers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.7-aquifers.mdx).

**Procedural carver.** The MC-style cave generator implemented in `carver.rs`. Each chunk independently seeds winding tunnels via `CarverRng(seed, chunk_coord, salt)`. Tunnels are traced as chains of ellipsoidal spheres and rasterised into a per-chunk boolean mask; the mask is stamped into `composed` via `composed = composed.min(-CAVE_SDF_INTENSITY)`. Currently the only active cave layer (`CAVE_SYSTEMS_PER_REGION = (0, 0)` disables graph caves). See [4.5 Procedural Carvers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.5-procedural-carvers.mdx).

### R

**Region cache.** The two-level LRU memoisation system: `FineCache` (256 entries, ~13–20 MB) for 512-block fine regions and `MacroCache` (32 entries, ~3.6 MB) for 8192-block macro regions. Both use `Arc<Mutex<LruCache<...>>>`. Cache misses rebuild outside the lock; duplicate builds are accepted because builds are pure. See [5.2 The Region Cache](../part-5-engineering/5.2-region-cache.mdx).

**Ridges_pv ("peaks and valleys").** A 2-octave Simplex FBM passed through a triangle-wave fold (`peaks_and_valleys`), converting the bipolar raw signal into one that is high at extreme raw values and low near zero. When high, the jaggedness spline can produce sharp ridgelines. One of the three axes driving the nested heightmap splines. Seeded at `seed + 404`. See [3.2 Climate](../part-3-region-build/3.2-climate.mdx).

**RiverSegment.** A cell-center-to-cell-center polyline step stored as `{ from: (i32, i32), to: (i32, i32), width: f32, mouth: bool }`. Built during fine region construction and stored in `FineRegion::segments`. At chunk fill, all segments from the 3×3 region neighbourhood are iterated to compute valley carve depth via the U-profile formula and meander warp. See [3.5 Rivers, Valleys, Lakes](../part-3-region-build/3.5-rivers-lakes.mdx).

**RON (Rusty Object Notation).** The serialization format used for `assets/worldgen/default.ron`. Chosen over JSON or TOML because it supports Rust-native enum syntax (e.g. `Constant(0.5)`, `Multipoint([...])`) and inline comments. All runtime-tunable worldgen parameters live in this file and hot-reload on save. See [5.3 Config & Hot-Reload](../part-5-engineering/5.3-config-hot-reload.mdx).

**RuleSource.** The data-driven surface rule type: a recursive enum of `Block(Block)`, `Sequence(Vec<RuleSource>)`, and `If { condition: ConditionSource, then: Box<RuleSource> }`. `Sequence` is short-circuit OR (first matching child wins). Stored as `WorldgenConfig::surface` and evaluated per-solid near-surface voxel. Fully hot-reloadable. See [4.6 Surface Rules](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.6-surface-rules.mdx).

---

### S

**Salt (in hash).** A trailing integer literal appended to the `words` slice in a `hash::mix*` call to make two rolls at the same coordinate uncorrelated. E.g. `mix(seed, &[cx, cy, cz, 0])` for X-jitter and `mix(seed, &[cx, cy, cz, 1])` for Y-jitter. Salts are inline literals at the call site, not named constants. See [1.2 Deterministic Randomness](../part-1-foundations/1.2-determinism.mdx).

**SDF (signed distance function).** A function assigning a scalar to every point: positive inside the shape, zero at the boundary, negative outside. In the engine SDFs are "soft": intensity tapers from 1 (deep inside) to 0 (boundary) using `max(0, 1 − ρ)` for ellipsoids and `max(0, 1 − dist/r)` for capsules. Cave volumes compose by `max` (union); carving applies by `min(density, −sdf)`. See [1.8 Signed Distance Functions](../part-1-foundations/1.8-sdfs.mdx).

**Sea level.** The world-Y coordinate (62) at which the ocean surface sits. Any column whose `h_target ≤ SEA_LEVEL` is an ocean column, filled with Water up to `SEA_LEVEL` by the surface flood. Aquifer surfaces are capped at `sea_level − 1` to prevent aquifer water emerging above the ocean. Defined in `tuning.rs`. See [2.1 The Big Picture](../part-2-overview/2.1-big-picture.mdx).

**Simplex noise.** The coherent noise primitive used throughout the engine (via the `noise` crate). A triangular-lattice refinement of Perlin noise: fewer gradient corners per cell in 3D (4 vs 8), faster to evaluate, and free of the grid-axis directional bias that Perlin exhibits. All `Fbm::<Simplex>` instances use this as their base. See [1.3 Coherent Noise](../part-1-foundations/1.3-coherent-noise.mdx).

**Sink fill.** See **Planchon–Darboux**.

**Slide function.** `heightmap::slide(density, wy, cfg)`: a post-evaluation clamp that forces the density near the world's vertical boundaries. Near `y_max`, density is lerped toward `slide_top_target` (negative — air wins). Near `y_min`, lerped toward `slide_bottom_target` (positive — solid wins). Below `y_min`, returns 100.0 unconditionally. Applied per-voxel after the cell evaluator, not inside the tree. See [4.1 The Density Tree](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.1-density-graph.mdx).

**Smoothstep.** The cubic polynomial `s(t) = t²(3 − 2t)`, used in the valley carve U-profile wall transition and in the surface entrance channel fade. Produces a smooth S-curve from 0 to 1 with zero derivative at both endpoints — the same shape as a cubic Hermite spline with both endpoint slopes set to zero. See [3.5 Rivers, Valleys, Lakes](../part-3-region-build/3.5-rivers-lakes.mdx).

**Spline (see Cubic Hermite, NestedSpline, Catmull-Rom).** General term for a piecewise-polynomial curve through a set of knots. The engine uses cubic Hermite splines everywhere for 1D mappings and nests them for multi-axis mappings. See [1.7 Splines](../part-1-foundations/1.7-splines.mdx).

**Stage (visualizer).** A variant of `worldgen::probe::Stage` selecting which pipeline value the map view in `worldgen_viz` renders: `Continentalness`, `PlateId`, `Temperature`, `Humidity`, `Weirdness`, `HPre`, `ValleyCarve`, `HTarget`, `FlowAccum`, `BiomeId`, `AquiferY`, `AquiferSubstance`. Each stage has a colormap in `viz_render::stages`. See [5.4 The Visualizer](../part-5-engineering/5.4-visualizer.mdx).

**Surface buffer (`CAVE_SURFACE_BUFFER`).** The depth-below-heightmap threshold (default −1) below which most cave carvers are enabled. A value of −1 means even the topmost solid block is carveable; raising it to a positive integer protects the top N solid layers from all depth-gated carvers (wormhole, cheese, spaghetti, pillars) while leaving the surface-entrance channel unrestricted. See [4.3 Composing Caves](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.3-composing-caves.mdx).

---

### T

**TargetPoint.** The biome lookup query struct: six `i64` values — `temperature`, `humidity`, `continentalness`, `terrain_shape`, `depth`, `weirdness`, `offset` — quantized from `f32` by multiplying by 10 000. Integer arithmetic makes comparisons byte-deterministic across platforms. The R-tree search finds the `ParameterPoint` with minimum squared-L2 distance-to-interval. See [3.2 Climate](../part-3-region-build/3.2-climate.mdx).

**TreeKind.** An enum `{ Oak, Palm }` selecting the canopy shape stamped by `stamp_tree`. Oak: roughly spherical Leaves shell, radius² threshold 6, Y range −1..+2 above trunk top. Palm: flat five-frond pattern at 72° intervals, 3 blocks per frond, outer tip drops 1 block. Assigned by biome: Tropical → Palm, all others → Oak. See [4.9 Trees](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.9-trees.mdx).

**Trilinear interpolation (trilerp).** The extension of bilinear interpolation to three dimensions. Given eight corner values $c_{xyz}$ for $x, y, z \in \{0, 1\}$, computed as two bilerps at $z = 0$ and $z = 1$ then a final lerp along $z$. Seven lerp operations total. Used by the `CellEvaluator` to fill the 31 039 non-corner voxels in a chunk from 729 sampled corners. See [1.9 Trilinear Interpolation](../part-1-foundations/1.9-trilerp.mdx).

**Tuning constants.** Compile-time `pub const` values in `src/worldgen/tuning.rs` controlling structural worldgen choices: `PLATE_CELL_SIZE`, `SEA_LEVEL`, `FINE_CACHE_CAP`, `RIVER_THRESH`, `RIVER_WIDTH_SCALE`, `CAVE_FLOOR_Y`, `TREE_CELL_SIZE`, cave band bounds, and more. Distinguished from runtime RON knobs — changing tuning constants requires recompilation. See [5.3 Config & Hot-Reload](../part-5-engineering/5.3-config-hot-reload.mdx).

---

### U

**Underground density threshold.** `cfg.cave.underground_density_threshold` (default 0.0 in `default.ron`). The cheese and spaghetti noise carvers only activate when `raw_density >= underground_density_threshold`, restricting them to voxels comfortably in the solid mass and preventing surface erosion. The surface-entrance carver deliberately skips this gate. See [4.3 Composing Caves](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.3-composing-caves.mdx).

---

### V

**Voronoi diagram.** A spatial partition where every point belongs to the region of its nearest seed. In the engine: plates (1024-block jittered cells), aquifer cells (16×12×16 jittered cells), and per-block biome edge jitter (quart-grid 4-block cells) all use jittered Voronoi. The boundary intensity field `t = (d_b − d_a) / (d_b + d_a)` gives a continuous scalar that is 0 on Voronoi edges and approaches 1 deep inside a cell. See [1.5 Voronoi Diagrams](../part-1-foundations/1.5-voronoi.mdx).

---

### W

**Weirdness.** A 2-octave Simplex FBM (seeded at `seed + 501`, period ~800 blocks) that serves as the "rare biome variant" axis, analogous to Minecraft's weirdness axis. Currently all biome entries span the full weirdness range, so it does not alter biome assignment in Phase 1, but the infrastructure is in place for future narrow-window entries (ice spikes, mushroom fields, etc.). See [3.2 Climate](../part-3-region-build/3.2-climate.mdx).

**WorldgenConfig.** The root struct for all hot-reloadable worldgen parameters, deserialised from `assets/worldgen/default.ron`. Sub-configs: `DensityConfig`, `ClimateConfig`, `CaveConfig`, `AquiferConfig`, `surface: RuleSource`, `BiomesConfig`. Held behind a `ConfigHolder(Arc<ArcSwap<WorldgenConfig>>)`. Each chunk snapshots the config once at the start of `fill_chunk` via `config_snapshot()` to keep the whole chunk deterministic even if the file watcher fires mid-generation. See [5.3 Config & Hot-Reload](../part-5-engineering/5.3-config-hot-reload.mdx).

**Wormhole noise.** A deep-band cave carver (`WormholeNoise`) that evaluates two independent 3D Simplex FBMs (seeded at `seed + 301` and `seed + 302`) and carves a voxel when both are near zero simultaneously — two zero-crossing sheets intersecting to form a tubular passage. Active only below `WORMHOLE_BAND_Y = −40`. Carves by `composed = composed.min(−CAVE_SDF_INTENSITY)`. See [4.3 Composing Caves](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.3-composing-caves.mdx).

---

### X

**Xor-shift.** The bit-manipulation step `h ^= h >> 33` used three times in the `hash::mix` finaliser to propagate high bits into low bits before each multiply. Combined with the large-prime multipliers, this achieves the full-avalanche property. See [1.2 Deterministic Randomness](../part-1-foundations/1.2-determinism.mdx).

---

### Y

**`y_gradient`.** The `DensityFn::YGradient` leaf: a linear ramp from `+y_gradient_amplitude` at `y_min` (strongly solid) to `−y_gradient_amplitude` at `y_max` (strongly air). The dominant term that ensures most voxels far from the surface are unambiguously solid or air; only a contested band near the surface threshold requires the full density composition. See [4.1 The Density Tree](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.1-density-graph.mdx).

**`y_top` (aquifer).** The world-Y coordinate of a given aquifer cell's fluid surface, placed in the lower third of the cell (`cy × AQUIFER_CELL_Y + AQUIFER_CELL_Y / 3`) with a ±6-block jitter and capped at `sea_level − 1`. Any carved voxel at or below `y_top` is a candidate for flooding, subject to the pressure model. See [4.7 Aquifers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.7-aquifers.mdx).
