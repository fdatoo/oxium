Every compile-time constant in `src/worldgen/tuning.rs` with its value and the chapter that explains why. Runtime-tunable parameters (spline curves, biome rates, surface rules) live in `assets/worldgen/default.ron` — see [5.3 Config & Hot-Reload](../part-5-engineering/5.3-config-hot-reload.mdx).

Derived constants (computed from other constants at compile time) appear in a final section.

## Plates

| Constant | Value | Description | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PLATE_CELL_SIZE` | `1024` | Edge length (blocks) of one Voronoi plate seed cell; smaller → more plates → finer continental shapes | [3.1 Plates](../part-3-region-build/3.1-plates.mdx) |
| `CONTINENTAL_RATIO` | `0.45` | Fraction of plates classified as continental; the remainder are oceanic, governing land:ocean ratio | [3.1 Plates](../part-3-region-build/3.1-plates.mdx) |
| `ROUGHNESS_RANGE` | `(0.7, 1.4)` | Per-plate additive bias range on the climate `terrain_shape` channel; mapped to `ClimateConfig::plate_roughness_bias_range` | [3.1 Plates](../part-3-region-build/3.1-plates.mdx) |
