A cross-reference from reusable techniques to every chapter where they appear. "Defined in" points to the chapter that builds the technique from first principles. "Used in" lists every other chapter that applies it.

| Technique | One-line intuition | Defined in | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Catmull-Rom spline** | Smooth curve through control points whose tangents are derived from neighbours, not authored explicitly | [1.7 Splines](../part-1-foundations/1.7-splines.mdx) (Hermite primer; Catmull-Rom is the tunnel variant, not the heightmap variant) | [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx) — 4-point control-point polyline stored per `Tunnel`; capsule segments at carve time approximate a Catmull-Rom path (not Hermite — tangents are implicitly derived from the stored neighbour points) |
| **Cubic Hermite spline** | Piecewise cubic curve whose slope at each knot is explicitly authored, giving direct control over transition steepness | [1.7 Splines](../part-1-foundations/1.7-splines.mdx) | [3.3 Heightmap](../part-3-region-build/3.3-heightmap.mdx) — three nested `NestedSpline` stacks (`offset`, `factor`, `jaggedness`) all evaluate via the Hermite formula; [4.1 Density Graph](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.1-density-graph.mdx) — same spline nodes inside the `DensityFn` tree |
| **D8 flow direction** | Each grid cell points to the steepest of its 8 neighbours (4 cardinal + 4 diagonal, distance-weighted) to build a drainage tree | [3.4 Hydrology](../part-3-region-build/3.4-hydrology.mdx) | [3.5 Rivers, Valleys, Lakes](../part-3-region-build/3.5-rivers-lakes.mdx) — `flow_dir` codes from the fine pass drive segment extraction; the macro pass runs the identical algorithm at coarser resolution |
| **Domain warping** | Sample a noise field at coordinates pre-shifted by another noise field, turning smooth blobs into swirling, bent shapes | [1.6 Domain Warping](../part-1-foundations/1.6-domain-warping.mdx) | [3.5 Rivers, Valleys, Lakes](../part-3-region-build/3.5-rivers-lakes.mdx) — `perpendicular_distance` adds a hash-interpolated perpendicular offset along each river segment (meander warp); [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx) — tunnel control points are pushed perpendicular to the `pa → pb` axis before the Catmull-Rom path is stored |
| **FBM / fractal Brownian motion** | Sum N octaves of coherent noise at doubling frequencies and halving amplitudes, producing detail at every scale | [1.4 FBM](../part-1-foundations/1.4-fbm.mdx) | [3.2 Climate](../part-3-region-build/3.2-climate.mdx) — temperature, humidity, weirdness, `terrain_shape`, `ridges_raw`, and `relief` FBMs; [3.3 Heightmap](../part-3-region-build/3.3-heightmap.mdx) — `terrain_shape` and `ridges_raw` FBMs feed the spline pipeline; [4.4 Noise Carvers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.4-noise-carvers.mdx) — `cheese`, `spaghetti_2d`, `spaghetti_2d_modulator`, `spaghetti_2d_elevation`, `spaghetti_2d_thickness`, `spaghetti_roughness`, `pillar`, `pillar_rareness`, `pillar_thickness`, and `surface_entrance` are all `Fbm<Simplex>`; [4.7 Aquifers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.7-aquifers.mdx) — 2-octave barrier FBM shapes the fluid-surface edge |
| **Hash mixer** | Pure function `mix(seed, &[coords, salt]) → u64` that maps integer inputs to uniformly distributed outputs with full avalanche | [1.2 Deterministic Randomness](../part-1-foundations/1.2-determinism.mdx) | Every discrete roll across all chapters — biome jitter, aquifer cell placement, plate property rolls, cave chamber placement, surface decoration, carver RNG seeding; audited comprehensively in [5.1 Determinism Everywhere](../part-5-engineering/5.1-determinism.mdx) |
| **LRU cache** | Bounded map that evicts the least-recently-used entry when full, amortising expensive builds across many consumers | [5.2 The Region Cache](../part-5-engineering/5.2-region-cache.mdx) | [4.5 Procedural Carvers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.5-procedural-carvers.mdx) — `carver_cache: LruCache<ChunkCoord, Arc<Vec<CarverTunnel>>>` (capacity 2048) avoids re-rolling tunnel geometry for each of the 605 neighbours swept per chunk fill |
| **MST / Kruskal's algorithm** | Sort all pairwise edges by weight; greedily add each edge that joins two disconnected components (union-find) to build a minimum spanning tree | [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx) | (sole use) — connects cave chambers into the minimum-total-length tunnel graph before extra loop edges are appended |
| **Planchon–Darboux sink fill** | Iteratively raise local minima until every interior cell has a non-decreasing path to the boundary, eliminating closed drainage sinks | [3.4 Hydrology](../part-3-region-build/3.4-hydrology.mdx) | [3.5 Rivers, Valleys, Lakes](../part-3-region-build/3.5-rivers-lakes.mdx) — cells whose filled height exceeds natural height are lake interiors; `lake_rim` from the PD pass becomes the water-surface elevation used during `fill_chunk`; the macro pass runs an identical PD step at coarse resolution |
| **Poisson-disk sampling** | Place points such that no two are closer than a minimum distance, yielding a natural-looking spread with no clumping or gaps | [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx) | (sole use) — rejection-sampling variant places cave chamber centres inside each system bounding box, with minimum spacing 3× mean ellipsoid radius |
| **Signed distance functions (SDFs)** | Scalar field positive inside a shape, zero at its boundary, negative outside; union = max, subtraction = min of negated | [1.8 SDFs](../part-1-foundations/1.8-sdfs.mdx) | [3.6 Cave Systems](../part-3-region-build/3.6-cave-systems.mdx) — chamber ellipsoid and tunnel capsule shapes are expressed as soft-intensity SDFs; [4.3 Composing Caves](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.3-composing-caves.mdx) — `cave_sdf` and `entrance_sdf` compose via `max` (union) then subtract from density via `min(-sdf)`; [4.4 Noise Carvers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.4-noise-carvers.mdx) — `sphere_carves` is an ellipsoidal SDF test; [4.5 Procedural Carvers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.5-procedural-carvers.mdx) — `sphere_carves` applies the same ellipsoid equation with separate horizontal and vertical radii |
| **Simplex noise** | Gradient noise on a triangular/tetrahedral lattice; fewer corners per cell than Perlin, fewer grid-axis artifacts | [1.3 Coherent Noise](../part-1-foundations/1.3-coherent-noise.mdx) | Everywhere `Fbm<Simplex>` appears — every noise field in the engine uses Simplex as the base primitive (see FBM row above for the full list) |
| **Smoothstep** | Hermite-interpolated S-curve `3t² − 2t³` with zero first derivative at both endpoints, giving a natural-looking ease-in/ease-out | [1.7 Splines](../part-1-foundations/1.7-splines.mdx) (as the cubic correction term when both slopes are zero) | [3.5 Rivers, Valleys, Lakes](../part-3-region-build/3.5-rivers-lakes.mdx) — U-profile valley walls use `s = 1 − t²(3 − 2t)` to taper carve depth from `RIVER_BED_DEPTH` to zero across the valley shoulder; [4.4 Noise Carvers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.4-noise-carvers.mdx) — `surface_entrance_contribution` uses `t²(3 − 2t)` to ramp the shaft intensity from threshold to full depth |
| **Trilinear interpolation** | Blend 8 corner values across a 3D cell using seven lerps (X then Y then Z), reducing 32 768 expensive evaluations per chunk to 729 | [1.9 Trilinear Interpolation](../part-1-foundations/1.9-trilerp.mdx) | [4.2 The Cell Evaluator](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.2-cell-evaluator.mdx) — `CellEvaluator` builds a 9×9×9 corner lattice then answers every per-voxel density query with 7 multiplies and 7 adds (~134× speedup) |
| **Voronoi diagram** | Partition space into cells, each owned by its nearest seed point; boundary intensity field `(d_b − d_a)/(d_b + d_a)` varies smoothly from 0 at edges to 1 at cell centres | [1.5 Voronoi Diagrams](../part-1-foundations/1.5-voronoi.mdx) | [3.1 Plates](../part-3-region-build/3.1-plates.mdx) — jittered-grid Voronoi partitions the world into continental and oceanic plates; boundary field drives ridge lift and shelf taper; [3.2 Climate](../part-3-region-build/3.2-climate.mdx) — per-block hash jitter perturbs `(temperature, humidity)` before biome lookup, producing irregular biome edges (note: the lookup uses an R-tree with fanout 6 over `Vec<ParameterPoint>` nodes, nearest-by-squared-L2); [4.7 Aquifers](../part-4-chunk-fill/4.7-aquifers.mdx) — 16×12×16 jittered cell grid, 27-cell neighbourhood sweep to find the three nearest cell centres for the pressure model |
| **Xor-shift / golden-ratio multiply** | Bit-mixing operations (`h ^= h >> 33; h = h.wrapping_mul(constant)`) that distribute input bits uniformly across all output bits (avalanche) | [1.2 Deterministic Randomness](../part-1-foundations/1.2-determinism.mdx) | [5.1 Determinism Everywhere](../part-5-engineering/5.1-determinism.mdx) — the three-step `(xor-shift, multiply, xor-shift)` finaliser in `hash::mix` is the avalanche step; audited as part of the purity verification |
